# Etsy Rooster Shop — Plan 2c: Posters via Nano Banana Pro

**Status:** Design approved 2026-05-20. Awaiting implementation plan (writing-plans).

**Predecessors:** Plan 1 (mandala SVG) + Plan 2a (coloring books + branded shop) shipped earlier today. Plan 2a' fast-follow added the Songbird draft and Mandala draft, both pending the user's dashboard work.

**Successor sub-plans:** Plan 2b (`pocket_rooster_brand` refactor), Plan 3 (validate-then-scale to N SKUs). Plan 2c' fast-follow: more posters in other niches once this pipeline is proven.

## Goal

Populate the empty "Printable Posters" shop section with **one** cottagecore-themed printable poster, end-to-end. Validates the poster pipeline at the smallest possible scope. After this, the shop has all three product types live: coloring books + SVG cut files + posters.

### Print sizes in the buyer ZIP

Five JPGs at 300 DPI:

| Size (in) | Pixels @ 300 DPI | Aspect | Crop strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 × 10 | 2400 × 3000 | 4:5 (= 0.800) | Center-crop the 3:4 (= 0.750) master |
| 11 × 14 | 3300 × 4200 | ~11:14 (= 0.786) | Center-crop the 3:4 master |
| 16 × 20 | 4800 × 6000 | 4:5 (= 0.800) | Center-crop then upscale via LANCZOS |
| 18 × 24 | 5400 × 7200 | 3:4 (= 0.750) | Direct upscale from 3:4 master |
| A4 | 2480 × 3508 | ~3:4.24 (= 0.708) | Center-crop the 3:4 master, slight horizontal trim |

All saved as JPG with `quality=92` (high-quality for print). Pillow's `save(..., dpi=(300, 300))` writes 300 DPI metadata so the buyer's print software shows the correct physical size.

### Print-instructions PDF (static one-pager)

Single page, US Letter portrait. reportlab-rendered with brass-on-cream palette (matches shop branding). Content:

```
THANK YOU FOR YOUR PURCHASE — Pocket Rooster Press

YOUR DOWNLOAD INCLUDES
  • 8 × 10 in
  • 11 × 14 in
  • 16 × 20 in
  • 18 × 24 in
  • A4 (21 × 29.7 cm)

All files are 300 DPI JPG, ready for home or commercial printing.

HOW TO PRINT
  • At home — print the JPG at the listed size on photo paper or matte cardstock.
  • Online services — upload the file directly to Walmart Photo, Costco Photo,
    Snapfish, Mpix, or your preferred print shop.
  • Local print shops — most copy shops can print these up to 18 × 24.

LICENSE
  For personal use only. Not for resale or commercial use.
  Designs are created with AI image tools and refined for clean printing,
  disclosed per Etsy's 2024 listing-quality policy.
```

Content is static — same PDF text for every poster. The bundle_builder generates it fresh for each poster (no caching needed; render is sub-second).

               "For personal use only. Not for resale or commercial use.")

Niche: {niche}
Artifact summary: {artifact_summary_json}

## Open decisions (resolve during implementation)

1. **Master resolution** — 4K (3072 × 4096) is the largest Nano Banana Pro produces. The 16×20 (4800×6000) and 18×24 (5400×7200) require upscaling. Pillow LANCZOS upscaling preserves sharpness reasonably well for printable art, but if the first poster looks blocky at 18×24, consider either (a) accepting blockiness at the largest sizes, (b) generating two masters (4K + a separately rendered larger crop), or (c) dropping 18×24. Easiest fallback: generate 4K, accept the upscale, iterate if quality is bad.
2. **`Craft type` listing attribute for posters under taxonomy 2078** — needs a live check via the Etsy listing UI to see which craft_type dropdown options are exposed. Best guess: "Painting" or "Other." Fold into the runbook.
3. **Whether to include 24×36** — skipped above. If user later requests it, the easiest path is a separate Nano Banana Pro render with a tighter subject framing, then upscale.
