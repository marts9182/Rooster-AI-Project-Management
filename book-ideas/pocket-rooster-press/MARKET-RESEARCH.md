## Key Market Truths

1. **Type × Theme × Audience is the winning formula.** Generic "Adult Word Search" loses; "Gardener's Word Search Large Print" wins.
2. **Large print + back-of-book answer key are non-negotiable** for the senior segment (the dominant buyer).
3. **Series sell.** Bestsellers are vol 1/2/3/N. Plan series of 5-6 from day one — buyers of Vol 1 hunt for Vol 2.
4. **Top performers price at $6.99-$8.99** paperback, deliberately *below* the $9-$19 ceiling.
5. **8.5x11 is industry standard.** 6x9 only works as a deliberate "travel/pocket" differentiator.
6. **The "puzzle books for adults" keyword sees 96,873 searches/mo** — combine high-volume keywords with niche long-tail ones.
7. **Top-1 puzzle book** as of research date: "Summer Word Search Large Print: 1200+ Large Print Word Search Puzzle Books For Adults & Seniors" — BSR 935, ~613 sales/mo, $6.99 price.

## Underserved Theme/Audience Gaps Identified

For our audience targeting (adults, seniors, niche hobbyists — kids excluded):

### 1. Kakuro Large Print for Seniors (HIGHEST PRIORITY GAP)
- 36,635 searches/mo, near-zero supply
- "Pocket Rooster Big Print Kakuro Vol. 1" → first-mover position
- Future product after we validate the pipeline with the three locked launch books

### 2. Themed Cryptograms (LAUNCH BOOK #2)
- 35,633 searches/mo, only ~18 competing listings
- Public-domain quote sources only:
  - **Marcus Aurelius — *Meditations*** (Stoic philosophy)
  - **Lao Tzu — *Tao Te Ching***
  - **Aesop's Fables**
  - **Benjamin Franklin — *Poor Richard's Almanack***
  - Future: KJV Bible (separate faith-based product)
  - Future: Shakespeare, Sun Tzu

### 3. Hobbyist Word Search (LAUNCH BOOK #3 = Gardener's)
- Passionate gift-givers, lower competition than generic word search
- Theme candidates:
  - **Gardening** (locked for launch)
  - Fishing
  - Birdwatching
  - Knitting/Crochet
  - Quilting
  - Woodworking
  - RV/Camping
  - Classic Cars
  - Baking

### 4. Decade Nostalgia (Baby Boomer)
- Strong gift market, high repeat-buyer rate
- 60s/70s/80s music, TV shows, films, brands, cars
- Crossword + word search formats both work
- Caution: brand names borderline; stick to era descriptors ("Songs of the 70s") not specific trademarks

### 5. Travel/Pocket-Size 6x9 (LAUNCH BOOK #1 = Travel Sudoku)
- Almost nobody does this format well — 6x9 framed as "travel" is a real differentiator
- Future: "Cruise Word Search," "Cabin Cryptograms," "Road Trip Sudoku Vol. 2-5"

### 6. Faith-based Evergreen
- Strong, stable segment; KJV Bible cryptograms reserved as future product
- Christian word search/crossword also strong

## Themes to AVOID (Trademark/Copyright Risk)

- Specific TV shows (Friends, Stranger Things, etc.)
- Sports teams
- Video game franchises
- Music artists (band names + song titles)
- Modern song lyrics
- Living-author quotes
- Movie quotes/characters

KDP **will pull the listing** and may suspend the account for repeat infringement.

---

## Competitive Strategy

1. **Match average pricing** ($6.99-$8.99) — stay slightly below niche average for psychological edge.
2. **Lead with the gap.** Title carries the audience+theme combo: "Travel Sudoku — Pocket Edition for Adults & Seniors."
3. **Brand consistency** — every Pocket Rooster Press book has the same cover layout grammar so Vol 2+ is recognizable.
4. **Series tease** in description — "Volume 1 of the Pocket Rooster Travel Sudoku series."
5. **Stack the listing** with audience benefit (large print, answer key, puzzle count) before features.
6. **No paid ads at launch** — organic ranking via 7 backend keywords + 2 niche categories.
