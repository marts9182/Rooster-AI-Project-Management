================================================================================
  LAST HAVEN — Colony Survival Roguelike
  Design Document
================================================================================

HIGH CONCEPT
A top-down roguelike colony survival game where you lead a band of shipwreck
survivors on a procedurally generated island. Build, assign roles, manage morale,
and defend against escalating nightly threats. Each colony wipe unlocks
persistent meta-progression.

GENRE & PLATFORM
- Genre:    Top-down survival / colony sim / roguelike
- Platform: PC (Steam), mobile (iOS/Android via Capacitor), web
- Engine:   HTML5 Canvas or Phaser 3
- Art:      Pixel art, 16x16 or 32x32 tiles, muted earthy palette with vibrant UI

KEY SYSTEMS
================================================================================

1. SURVIVOR MANAGEMENT
   - Each survivor has: Name, HP, Hunger, Morale, 3 Skills
     (Farming, Combat, Crafting, Scouting, Medicine — each 1-10)
   - Survivors gain XP in the role you assign them
   - Low morale -> desertion, fights, refusal to work
   - Survivors can die permanently; you recruit new ones from random events

2. ROLE ASSIGNMENT (Morning Phase)

   Role       Action                    Output
   -------    ----------------------    --------------------------
   Farmer     Tends crops               Food per skill level
   Scout      Explores fog of war       Map reveals, event triggers
   Builder    Constructs/repairs         Building progress
   Crafter    Creates tools/weapons      Items
   Guard      Patrols perimeter          Early raid warnings, traps armed
   Medic      Heals injured survivors    HP restoration

3. BASE BUILDING
   - Grid-based placement on the island
   - Structures: Shelter (morale), Walls (defense), Farm Plots, Workshop,
     Watchtower, Medical Tent, Storage
   - Structures degrade nightly from raids; must be repaired
   - Unlock tiers: Thatch -> Wood -> Stone -> Reinforced

4. NIGHTLY RAIDS
   - Auto-battler style: you set positions and gear, survivors fight automatically
   - Enemy types escalate:
       Wolves (Day 1-5)
       Bandits (6-15)
       Mutants (16-30)
       Eldritch Horrors (31+)
   - Raid intensity scales with colony "prosperity score" (more stuff = bigger raids)
   - Terrain matters: walls funnel enemies, watchtowers give range, traps deal AOE

5. EXPLORATION
   - Island is fog-of-war, revealed tile by tile
   - Biomes: Beach, Forest, Swamp, Mountains, Ruins, Caves
   - Points of Interest: Shipwrecks (loot), Shrines (buffs), Caves (dungeons),
     NPC camps (trade/recruit)
   - Each run generates a new island (seed-based)

6. META PROGRESSION (Persistent)
   - "Legacy Points" earned based on: days survived, buildings built, enemies
     killed, events completed
   - Unlock: Starting bonuses (extra survivor, bonus tools), new building
     blueprints, cosmetic island themes
   - Lore entries unlock as you discover things — builds overarching mystery
     of why the island exists

================================================================================
RESOURCE ECONOMY
================================================================================

   Resource    Source                 Used For
   ---------   -------------------    ----------------------------------
   Wood        Forest tiles           Buildings, tools, fuel
   Stone       Mountain tiles         Advanced buildings, weapons
   Food        Farms, foraging        Daily consumption (1/survivor/day)
   Herbs       Swamp/forest           Medicine, morale potions
   Scrap       Ruins, shipwrecks      Advanced crafting, traps
   Crystal     Deep caves             Endgame tech, shrine activation

================================================================================
WIN / LOSS CONDITIONS
================================================================================

- Loss: All survivors dead or all desert (morale 0)
- "Win": Survive 30 days and activate the island's central shrine (requires
  full exploration + rare resources). Reveals story ending. Multiple endings
  based on choices.
- Endless Mode: No shrine goal, pure survival for leaderboard score
