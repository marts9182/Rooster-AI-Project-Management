================================================================================
  THE EIGHTH BELL — Time-Loop Detective RPG
  Design Document
================================================================================

HIGH CONCEPT
You're a detective trapped in a 24-hour time loop in a sprawling fantasy city.
Every loop resets the world but you keep your knowledge. NPCs live full
schedules. Crimes unfold whether you witness them or not. Solving the central
mystery requires weaving together clues from dozens of loops, making allies,
and breaking the cycle.

GENRE & PLATFORM
- Genre:    Narrative RPG / mystery / time-loop puzzle
- Platform: PC (Steam), potential mobile port
- Engine:   Phaser 3 or custom HTML5 (top-down 2D)
- Art:      Detailed pixel art, 32x32 characters, painterly backgrounds.
            Noir-fantasy aesthetic — gas lamps, cobblestone, magic runes.

================================================================================
CORE LOOP
================================================================================

  Loop Starts (6:00 AM)
  |
  +-- Explore the city, talk to NPCs
  +-- Follow leads, gather clues
  +-- Use abilities to access new areas/info
  +-- Witness or intervene in events
  |
  +-- KEY EVENTS happen at fixed times regardless of player
  |   (Murder at 2PM, Heist at 8PM, Ritual at 11PM, etc.)
  |
  +-- Midnight -> Loop Resets
      +-- You keep: Knowledge, clue journal, unlocked abilities
      +-- World resets: NPCs, items, events, relationships (partial)

THE CITY: BELLHAVEN
================================================================================

Districts (6):

   District        Flavor                 Key Locations
   ------------    -------------------    ------------------------------------
   Old Quarter     Medieval, cramped      Tavern, Thieves' Guild, Black Market
   Temple Row      Religious, ornate      Cathedral, Monastery, Oracle's Chamber
   The Docks       Rough, industrial      Warehouses, Smugglers' Cove, Fish Mkt
   Highwall        Wealthy, guarded       Manor Houses, Council Hall, Gardens
   Academy         Scholarly, magical     Library, Alchemy Lab, Clocktower
   Undercity       Hidden, dangerous      Catacombs, Sewer Network, Shrine

================================================================================
TIME SYSTEM
================================================================================

- 24-hour day divided into 18 "time units" (each ~80 minutes in-world)
- Moving between districts costs 1-2 time units
- Conversations cost 0-1 time units depending on depth
- Key events are scripted to specific times — you can't be everywhere at once
- Player must choose: "Do I follow the suspect to the docks at 2PM, or
  eavesdrop on the council meeting at 2PM?" -> requires multiple loops

================================================================================
NPC SCHEDULE SYSTEM
================================================================================

Every named NPC (30-40 total) has a full daily schedule:

   Example — Captain Maren Holt:
     06:00  Wakes at barracks
     07:00  Morning patrol (Old Quarter)
     09:00  Reports to Council Hall
     11:00  Lunch at Highwall cafe (meets contact)
     13:00  Investigates warehouse (IMPORTANT: finds evidence)
     15:00  Confrontation at docks <- CAN DIE HERE
     17:00  Returns to barracks OR dead
     20:00  Drinks at tavern (if alive, reveals info when drunk)

- Schedules branch based on whether the player intervened in prior events
- NPCs remember nothing between loops, but their behavior can be ALTERED
  within a loop by player actions

================================================================================
KNOWLEDGE & CLUE SYSTEM
================================================================================

THE JOURNAL (Persistent Across Loops):
- Clues auto-record when discovered
- Categories: People, Places, Events, Objects, Secrets
- Connections form automatically when related clues are found
  (conspiracy board style)
- Some clues unlock dialogue options in future loops
  ("I know you were at the warehouse at 1PM")
- "Knowledge gates": certain NPCs only reveal info if you demonstrate
  existing knowledge

EXAMPLE CLUE CHAIN:
  Loop 1: Overhear mention of "the eighth bell" at tavern
  Loop 2: Find a book about Bellhaven's missing 8th clocktower bell in library
  Loop 3: Ask the clockmaker about the bell -> he panics, runs to Temple Row
  Loop 4: Follow the clockmaker -> he meets a hooded figure at the Cathedral
  Loop 5: Identify the hooded figure using info from the thieves' guild
  Loop 6: Confront the figure with enough evidence -> major revelation

================================================================================
ABILITY SYSTEM
================================================================================

Instead of combat stats, abilities unlock new investigative tools:

   Ability          Unlock Condition           Effect
   -------------    -----------------------    ---------------------------------
   Eavesdrop        Start                      Listen to nearby conversations
   Persuade         5 clues about a person     New dialogue branches
   Lockpick         Find thieves' guild        Access locked doors/chests
   Arcane Sight     Library research chain     See magical residue/hidden runes
   Disguise         Befriend theater troupe    Enter restricted areas
   Time Echo        Major story milestone      "See" what happened 1 hour ago
   Loop Anchor      Endgame                    Carry ONE physical item across loops

================================================================================
DIALOGUE SYSTEM
================================================================================

- Keyword-based with skill checks
- Dialogue options are gated by: clues known, abilities unlocked, relationship
- Some NPCs have trust meters (reset each loop, but you know faster routes)
- Confrontation mechanic: Present evidence to NPCs -> they react based on
  how much proof you have. Wrong accusations waste time; right ones unlock
  new paths.

================================================================================
THE CENTRAL MYSTERY (Spoiler-Free Structure)
================================================================================

  Layer 1: A murder happens every loop at 2PM. Who did it?
           (Solvable in ~5 loops)

  Layer 2: The murder is connected to a larger conspiracy. Who's behind it?
           (~15 loops)

  Layer 3: The conspiracy is trying to prevent/cause something at midnight.
           What? (~25 loops)

  Layer 4: Why does the time loop exist? Who created it? What's "the eighth
           bell"? (~35 loops)

  Layer 5: Breaking the loop requires a specific sequence of actions.
           Multiple endings. (~40+ loops)
