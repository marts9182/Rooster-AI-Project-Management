# Your KDP Author URL

Your **Amazon Author Central page** is the public author profile Amazon
hosts for you across the US, UK, and DE storefronts. The dashboard uses
this URL as a fallback link target for Pinterest pins and as a quick deep
link from the Profile page out to your author hub.

## How to claim Author Central (one-time)

If you have not done this yet, do it before trying to grab the URL — the
claim takes one business day to approve.

1. Go to <https://author.amazon.com>.
2. Sign in with the same Amazon account you use for KDP.
3. Search for one of your own published books and confirm you are the
   author. Amazon emails an approval confirmation within ~24 hours.

## How to find the URL

1. Sign in to <https://author.amazon.com>.
2. Click your name in the top-right of the page.
3. You land on your **Profile** tab. Look for **Author page** in the
   left sidebar — clicking it opens your public storefront in a new tab.
4. Copy the URL from the address bar. It looks like
   `https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B0XXXXXXXX` (yes, the path
   includes an ASIN-shaped author identifier).

Paste that URL into the **KDP Author URL** field on the Profile page and
click **Save**.
