# Your Etsy shop URL

The dashboard uses your **Etsy shop URL** to deep-link the Etsy catalog
table back to your storefront and to build pin landing pages when you
queue Pinterest pins for Etsy listings.

## How to find it

1. Sign in to <https://www.etsy.com>.
2. In the top-right header, click your shop icon (or the **You** menu).
3. From the dropdown, choose **Shop Manager**.
4. In the left sidebar of Shop Manager, click **Settings**, then
   **About your shop** (or **Info & Appearance** on older Etsy UI).
5. Near the top of the page you will see your **Shop URL**. It looks
   like `https://www.etsy.com/shop/YourShopName`.

You can also just visit your live shop directly and copy the URL from
the browser bar — open Etsy, click your avatar, choose **Visit your
shop**, and copy whatever the address bar shows.

## What gets stored

The dashboard stores the URL exactly as you paste it. If you include a
trailing slash, query parameters, or tracking tags, those are kept. For
the cleanest deep links, paste the canonical
`https://www.etsy.com/shop/YourShopName` form with no trailing slash.
